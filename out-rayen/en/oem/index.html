How it runs

  1. 01

    Send a drawing or a sample

    A drawing is ideal — DWG, PDF or a photograph. Without one, a sample does the job just as well. What matters is that the mounting face, the hole positions and the spindle size are clear.

  2. 02

    We confirm the structure and dimensions

    You get back a checklist: material, backset, door thickness range, plate size, finish. Where we are unsure we ask, rather than assume the common size.

  3. 03

    New tooling, or a change to existing tooling

    Close to a tool we already have and a modification does it; a different structure needs a new tool. Cost and lead time are quoted per part, never from a table.

  4. 04

    Sample approval

    The sample goes to you and onto a real door before anything is decided. Changing it at this stage is far cheaper than changing it after production.

  5. 05

    Production and packing

    Packed to your requirement, including your brand, colour box, instructions and barcode. Carton data before production starts.
