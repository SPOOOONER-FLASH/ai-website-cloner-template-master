## 二、逐家明细

### Von Duprin (Allegion)

- **产地**：United States
- **对照系列**：98/99 Series rim exit device
- **持有**：
  - ANSI/BHMA A156.3-2014 Grade 1
  - ANSI/UL 305 (panic hardware)
  - ANSI/UL 10C (fire-rated configurations)
  - CAN/ULC-S104 (fire-rated configurations)
  - CAN/ULC-S132

> Fire-rated variants are ordered by adding an -F suffix; the fire listing belongs to that configuration, not to the series.

来源：<https://www.vonduprin.com/en/products/exit-devices/98-99-series.html>、<https://us.allegion.com/content/dam/allegion-us-2/web-files/von-duprin-/technical-documents/VON_98_99_Series_HPD_115784.pdf>

### Accentra (formerly Yale) (ASSA ABLOY)

- **产地**：United States
- **对照系列**：7100 Series wide-stile rim exit device
- **持有**：
  - ANSI/BHMA A156.3 Type 1, Grade 1
  - UL and cUL panic exit listing (FVSR / FVSR7)
  - UL fire exit hardware listing on -F variants (GXHX / GXHX7), 3 hour
- **页面上公开的规格**：
  - ANSI Function 01 — exit only, no trim
  - Latchbolt: 19mm (3/4") deadlocking stainless steel pullman
  - Minimum stile width 114mm (4-1/2")
  - Door thickness 44mm (1-3/4")
  - Projection 83mm active, 70mm dogged
  - Fire listing sizes: 4' x 8' single, 8' x 8' pairs
  - Five-year limited mechanical warranty

来源：<https://www.trudoor.com/yale/7100-rim-exit-device/>、<https://nationallocksupply.com/accentra-yale-7100-wide-stile-rim-exit-device/>

### Cal-Royal

- **产地**：not stated by the sources consulted
- **对照系列**：9800 / A9800 / F9800 rim and surface vertical rod
- **持有**：
  - ANSI/BHMA A156.3 Grade 1
  - UL and ULC listed for panic
  - UL fire rated up to 3 hours (F variants)
- **页面上公开的规格**：
  - ADA models operate at 5 lbf
  - Doors up to 8' x 8'
  - Brass, bronze or stainless rail assemblies and end caps

> Closest commercial analogue to our position: a US brand competing on Grade 1 certification rather than on being American-made. Country of manufacture is not published, which is itself informative — the certification is what is sold, not the origin.

来源：<https://www.cal-royal.com/files/04-20-Catalog-Exit-Devices-9800-2.pdf>、<https://www.trudoor.com/cal-royal/9800eo-f9800-panic-rim-exit-device/>

### Detex

- **产地**：New Braunfels, Texas, United States
- **对照系列**：Exit control locks and exit devices
- **持有**：
  - UL 305 (panic hardware)
  - UL 10C (fire door)
  - Warnock Hersey fire exit certification
  - ANSI/BHMA A156.3 Grade 1 where applicable
  - California State Fire Marshal listing 4140-0127:102
  - Miami-Dade County hurricane NOA 15-0603.15 (some models)

> The only maker in this set publishing the two REGIONAL approvals: California SFM and Miami-Dade NOA. Both are gates on real projects — Florida coastal work and California public buildings — and neither is satisfied by UL 305 alone.

来源：<https://www.detex.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Exit-Control-Locks.pdf>、<https://usmadesupply.com/companies/detex>

### Klacci (I-TEK Company)

- **产地**：Taiwan
- **对照系列**：30 Series and 1000 Series exit devices
- **持有**：
  - ANSI/BHMA A156.3-2014 Grade 1 (cycle test)
  - ANSI/UL 305
  - UL/cUL listed panic hardware, or 3-hour fire rated exit hardware
  - UL 10C and UBC 7-2 (fire exit devices)
  - ISO 9001, system certified by Underwriters Laboratories
  - ANSI/BHMA A156.2, A156.5 and A156.13 on other product lines
  - BHMA registered member
- **页面上公开的规格**：
  - Complies with NFPA 80 and NFPA 101
  - Meets 2010 ADA Standards 11B-309.4 and California Building Code 11B-309.4
  - Under 5 lbf push force to exit on accessible models
  - Centre-case latch throw 19mm (3/4"), top and bottom latches 16mm (5/8")
  - Door thickness 1-3/4" to 2-1/4", doors to 4' x 10'
  - Device centreline 39-5/8" from finished floor
  - BHMA finishes 613 and 628

> THE BENCHMARK FOR US. An Asian factory that holds exactly the certifications the American brands hold, and sells into the same specifications. Their in-house test facility complies with ISO 17025 and is certified by Intertek under a continuous re-inspection programme — that is the structure, not just the certificate.

来源：<https://www.klacci.com/30-series-exit-devices/>、<https://www.klacci.com/about-us/>

### Canton Hyland / HYDE

- **产地**：Zhongshan, Guangdong, China
- **对照系列**：300 series panic exit devices
- **持有**：
  - ISO 9001 since 2002
  - EN 1125 test report — Intertek Testing Services Shenzhen (Guangzhou branch), report 130722068GZU-001, 7 November 2013, model KD070/30-290
  - Tubular door lock durability test — Intertek, report 140306043GZU-001, 28 April 2014, model 607 SS ET
  - CE Certificate of Conformity to EN 1125:2008 — CELAB, Italy, 2010, panic exit device series
- **明确不持有**：
  - ANSI/BHMA A156.3 of any grade — the client confirmed this outright and src/data/company.ts forbids any product record carrying an ANSI grade
  - UL 305 panic hardware listing
  - UL 10C fire exit hardware listing
  - Any California SFM or Miami-Dade approval

> The three reports are real and are Canton Hyland's own — the EN 1125 report names Canton Hyland as both applicant and manufacturer with HYLAND as the trade mark. The SCANS are withheld because both issuers restrict redistribution and require written approval before their name is used in advertising. Stating standard, issuer, report number, model and date is not redistribution and is already public on /certifications.
