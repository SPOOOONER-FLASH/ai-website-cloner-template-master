# Catalogue Specification

- **Sources:** Canton product taxonomy and names; FSB product-collection layout rhythm only.
- **Routes:** `/products/` and `/products/[category]/`.
- **Desktop structure:** 24 columns, 42px column gaps, 1376px content band.
- **Overview:** two-column introduction followed by a flat category grid.
- **Category page:** breadcrumb, two-column introduction, sub-category filter and product grid.
- **Cards:** square media, 1px `--color-line` border, 0–2px radius, no shadow.
- **Interaction:** links and selected filters may use brand red; all headings, metadata and rules remain ink/grey.
- **Data rule:** omit prices, MOQ and unverified claims. Empty categories remain visible with an honest empty state.
- **Responsive:** cards collapse from four columns; P9 owns the final mobile pass.
