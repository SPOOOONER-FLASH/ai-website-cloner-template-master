2026-09-27 · Codex read-only audit. This note does not approve a replacement dimension or a product mesh. It records what the existing source images actually show so catalogue owners can correct the data without copying a guessed value across the family.

## What is affected now

- The five HYDE product pages at `/products/glass-door-accessories/{100,102,104,106,107}-glass-door-handle/` and their `/es/` and `/pt/` mirrors display specifications from `content/products/*.json` and a generated “Dimensioned drawing”. `src/components/site/ProductDetail.tsx` mounts `ProductDrawing`; `public/images/drawings/index.json` marks all five `partial: false`, and the component labels them “Drawn 1:1 from the published specification”.
- The five public SVG assets at `/images/drawings/{model}-glass-door-handle.svg` each returned HTTP 200 with `image/svg+xml` on 2026-09-27. This confirms exposure of the assets, not byte identity with the current checkout.
- `docs/collaboration/2026-09-15-modelling-shortlist.md` and `scripts/audit-modelling-candidates.mjs --family=tube` call all five complete, model-ready exteriors. The audit tests only the presence of five spec labels. It does not verify their meaning against the source diagrams or account for model-specific parts.

`Glass gap` values of 7/7.5/8 mm have ambiguous reference points in the drawings; do not relabel them as clearance to the installed glass until verified from a side/section view. `Tube Thickness` and optional variants are catalogue claims, not measurements visible in these elevations. ## Why the generated drawings cannot stand as measured product outlines

`scripts/build-dimension-drawings.mjs` feeds the catalogue `Centre distance` value into the **vertical** separation of its two horizontal connector bars (`pullHandle`, around lines 290–304). For example `public/images/drawings/102-glass-door-handle.svg` places them 147 drawing units apart, while the source image labels the 147 mm measure horizontally across both vertical tubes and the vertical connector pitch as 300 mm. The SVG also treats `Fixing centre = 30mm` as part of a 60 mm horizontal projection, even though the source 102 drawing has no such 60 mm projection. The same template is used for the five published SVGs, so a nominal “1:1” label is materially misleading. The separate door-prep index does not contain these five models; no drilled-hole pattern is confirmed here.

## Smallest safe next step

1. Catalogue owners should compare the five **original** dimensioned images above against their actual factory drawings. Correct the mislabeled horizontal outside width, the Ø26 connector diameter, and the 300-versus-30 pitch where directly established. Ask the factory to resolve 104/106 pitch, depth/glass clearance, fixings and side sections. Preserve the source images and explicitly distinguish optional wall-thickness variants.
2. Until the corrected facts and drawing generator are both verified, omit these five generated SVG “Dimensioned drawing” blocks from the product pages or replace them with the unchanged source diagram explicitly labeled as an unverified supplier diagram. Do not merely change the text while the geometry stays wrong. Add a regression check comparing each generated axis and marked dimension with the verified source mapping.
3. Do **not** publish a complete Blender hardware mesh for these five on the present evidence. The straight tube envelope for 100/102 could be shown separately as a partial dimensional study with unmodeled connectors/ends disclosed; it is not orderable CAD. The immediately safe Blender deliverable is a photographic source layout: `scripts/blender/hardware-source-layout.py` packs the actual Product Finder images unchanged as texture planes and explicitly records `isProductCad: false`. This approach preserves the photographed rings, fasteners, finishes and acrylic without inventing them.
