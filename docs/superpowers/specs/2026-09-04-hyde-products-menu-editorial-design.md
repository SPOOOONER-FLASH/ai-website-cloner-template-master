# HYDE products, menu, and editorial-image direction

## Decision already made

Use Scheme A for the approved image replacement: one neutral product-family atlas and one
credible, densely populated exhibition sample wall. They perform different jobs and must
not become two interchangeable trade-show photographs.

## Editorial asset library

Produce ten 3:2 product-photography compositions and ten 3:2 exhibition-wall compositions.
The set is concept/editorial material, not exact SKU evidence. Reject reversed hardware,
impossible mounting, oversized mechanisms, duplicated identical hinges, false marks,
readable fake labels, certification symbols, logos, or unsupported exhibition claims.

## Products decision previews

Prepare four desktop landscape previews in the current HYDE design world:

1. Opening System Map — a category map around a door opening.
2. Family Atlas — varied editorial range boards with a consistent photography grammar.
3. Door Condition — application-led entry with direct catalogue escape routes.
4. System Atlas + Designed Series — recommended fusion of the product map, family atlas,
   and a restrained designer/engineering-story layer without inventing named designers.

All versions preserve ordinary category/product anchors, EN/ES parity, and direct paths to
Finder, compare, configurator, downloads, and contact. This round chooses a direction; it
does not replace the production page yet.

## Menu decision previews

Prepare four desktop landscape previews in the current HYDE design world:

1. Specify / Source / Support — recommended; concise links plus two editorial routes.
2. Factory Proof — navigation plus a compact verified-fact strip.
3. Applications First — visual project needs, with all families behind disclosure.
4. RFQ Concierge — route visitors by model certainty and sourcing task.

The final menu must be less crowded than the current drawer, preserve mobile category
access, correct locale-sensitive paths, trap focus, restore focus on close, and keep email
and Product Finder prominent.

## Approval boundary

Show all eight previews to Spooner. Do not implement a Products or menu structure until a
direction is selected or Spooner explicitly delegates the choice. The two approved Scheme A
editorial replacements and the reusable 20-image library may proceed now.
