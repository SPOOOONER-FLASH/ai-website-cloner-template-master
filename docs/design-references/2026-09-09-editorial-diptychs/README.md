## Final prompt set

All four prompts used the supplied image as a style, tonal-restraint and diptych-composition
reference only. Shared requirements: photorealistic architectural-editorial photography; equal
panels separated by a narrow pure-white gutter; restrained natural light; real surface texture;
no text, logo, watermark, people, furniture, recognizable landmark or identifiable hardware.

### 01 — coastal limestone / stainless

Original Mediterranean coastal residence in pale local limestone and lime plaster, a deep empty
timber doorway with no visible fittings, dry grasses and distant sea under diffuse morning light.
Pair with an extreme macro of a plain brushed stainless sheet corner showing authentic linear
grain and one folded edge. Chalk, sand, muted olive and silver; no glossy CGI or luxury-ad props.

### 02 — concrete / graphite

Original board-formed-concrete courtyard and long covered passage after light rain, with a single
empty rectangular doorway. Pair with a macro of two plain graphite powder-coated planes meeting
at a precise folded seam, with subtle fine texture. Cool north light; no neon, sci-fi architecture
or exaggerated depth of field.

### 03 — oak / bronze

Original serene threshold of quarter-sawn oak, travertine and courtyard daylight, with a tall
plain opening and no visible fittings. Pair with a macro of an unperforated satin-brushed bronze
sheet with naturally varied brown-gold patina and one softly rolled edge. Warm indirect light;
no opulent styling, heavy vignette or product-advertising gloss.

### 04 — civic stone / aluminum

Original contemporary civic entrance of pale bush-hammered stone fins and a shadowed portico,
without signage or fittings. Pair with a macro of plain clear-anodized aluminum, fine horizontal
grain and a precise chamfered edge catching one line of light. Crisp neutral daylight; no flags,
monumental propaganda, dramatic clouds or wide-angle distortion.
