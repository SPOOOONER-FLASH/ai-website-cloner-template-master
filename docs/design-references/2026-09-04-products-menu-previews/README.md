# HYDE Products + hamburger decision previews

These eight previews are decision artifacts, not approved production UI. They apply the approved FSB-derived principles—system before SKU, one repeatable photographic grammar, and separate scope/application/technical image roles—without copying FSB assets, labels or brand treatment.

## Products

1. **Family Atlas — recommended.** One complete image answers range scope; HTML below explains the family hierarchy and next action. This is the cleanest direct replacement for a generic product-card wall.
2. **Category Map.** A permanent nine-family index sits beside the atlas. Strongest when quick category access matters more than an editorial opening.
3. **Designed as a System.** A reusable “Designed By” analogue without inventing external designers: four coordinated families, one photographic grammar, one maker line—“Engineered by Canton Hyland.”
4. **Family to Installed Opening.** A three-part narrative assigns overview, application and technical images separate jobs. Strongest teaching model, but longer than the other openings.

## Hamburger menu

1. **Specify / Source / Company — recommended.** Closest to FSB’s calm full-screen split. Two useful images support a short buyer-job information architecture; the email and enquiry action remain visible.
2. **Product Families.** The atlas becomes the visual index, with only five primary links and two small utility lists. Best for product-led navigation.
3. **Applications First.** One documentary wall and four opening types lead. Best for architects and schedule-driven buyers; weaker for visitors with a known model number.
4. **RFQ Concierge.** Three buyer states replace a traditional sitemap as the primary choice. Best conversion idea, but the largest behavioral departure and therefore the highest validation risk.
