All four outputs used built-in ImageGen with the user-supplied exhibition photographs as display-architecture references and first-party HYDE product files as subject references.

```text

Asset type: offline concept for a B2B architectural-hardware website homepage hero
Primary request: Create a new, original, physically believable architectural-hardware trade-show scene inspired only by the freestanding display-tower architecture in reference image 1. Do not copy the booth or brand. Show two elegant freestanding display monoliths in a quiet exhibition space: one monolith demonstrates the exact silver panic exit device from reference image 2 at full realistic scale; the second demonstrates the exact black exterior trim and black tubular lever set from references 3 and 4 as separate products, not as a claimed compatible kit.
Scene/backdrop: refined neutral-charcoal and warm-grey exhibition booth, soft grey carpet, discreet black ceiling truss, no factory imagery, no people in foreground, no lounge furniture.
Style/medium: high-end real architectural photography, not CGI, truthful metal textures and mounting hardware.
Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape, camera at human chest height, three-quarter angle, corrected verticals, 35mm lens feel, products and physical rack construction clearly readable, generous clean negative space on the left for later HTML copy.
Lighting/mood: soft exhibition spotlights with controlled highlights, natural shadows, no HDR glow.
Color palette: charcoal, warm grey, black, brushed silver; one finish per display grouping.
Constraints: Preserve the identifiable geometry and proportions of all referenced HYDE products. Product sets remain physically separate. The display monoliths must look buildable with visible bases, panels, and mounting points. No logos, no brand names, no product labels, no readable certification marks, no claims, no text, no watermark.
Avoid: competitor booth identity, fake HYDE attendance evidence, invented mechanisms, extra holes, random handles, mixed finishes, floating products, impossible supports, distorted wide-angle perspective, glossy 3D-render look, clutter.
```
