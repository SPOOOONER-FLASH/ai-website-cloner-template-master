## The truthful route to FSB-level continuity

Prefer a controlled real turntable or short camera arc of one physical HYDE product with stable softboxes, locked exposure/white balance/focus, fine matte field and verified product identity. This requires actual shot footage; existing sales-operation video is authentic but has hands, white cloth, framing and lighting that cannot be erased by a premium brief.

Alternatively use a factory-verified full model with front/back/side and dimensions for mounting faces, holes, latch, screws and internal pieces that will be visible. Validate orthographic geometry first, then broad area lights, material calibration against actual photos, slow 10–15 second angle study and restrained copy. Existing envelope/image-plane models are **not** an accurate lock assembly and must not be sold as one.

AI image-to-video may reshape small fixed metal details, so do not use it to synthesize unseen faces or mechanical operation for SKU evidence. A still-based move is deliberately less ambitious, but stays faithful to the product. Original real 305 operation footage can be a separate technical clip; do not splice its claims/colourway into another model or fake an installed door scene.
