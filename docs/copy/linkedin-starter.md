## 首发帖 1：锁芯长度怎么算（带工具）

A euro cylinder is ordered by two numbers, and getting them the wrong way round is the commonest ordering error in door hardware.

Each half is measured from the center of the fixing screw to the outer face of the escutcheon on that side. Half-lengths come in 5mm steps from a minimum of 27.5mm, so the figure is always rounded up: a half that is a little long can be covered with a rose, a half that is short cannot be fixed at the door.

Worked example: a 40mm door with 4mm escutcheons each side comes to 27.5mm + 27.5mm = 55mm, and the nearest length we publish is 56mm.

We turned the arithmetic into a calculator. Enter the door thickness and the escutcheon on each side:

## 首发帖 2：EN 1125 还是 EN 179

EN 1125 or EN 179? The choice is made by asking about the people, not about the importance of the door.

EN 1125 covers panic exit devices, a horizontal bar or touch bar, for people who may not know the building. EN 179 covers emergency exit devices, a lever or push pad, for people who know the building and the exit.

A rarely used fire exit in a stadium is EN 1125. A main door in a small office may be EN 179.

Get it the wrong way round and you can have an opening that is fully specified, properly installed and non-compliant. The occupancy test, the classification digits and the three common mistakes are here:
https://cantonlock.com/guides/en-1125-vs-en-179-2026/
