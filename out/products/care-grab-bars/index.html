Care Grab Bars

Grab bars and a fold-down shower seat for accessible bathrooms: straight, knurled and angled bars fixed to the wall, and bars that flip up out of the way.

Product data shown here is limited to verified client records. Additional references from the legacy catalog are being prepared for structured publication.
