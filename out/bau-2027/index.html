BAU 2027 · Munich

Meet us at BAU 2027, Hall C4, Stand 523.

From 11 to 15 January 2027 we are at Messe München with the exit devices, lock cases, night latches and master key cylinders buyers ask us about most. Book a time at the stand, and bring your drawings or the model numbers you buy today.

What we are bringing

The models below have their own pages with spec tables and photographs. At the stand you can hold them, check the fixing points against your door, and ask for samples in the finish you need.

  • 307 panic exit devicealuminum alloy body with a 1000mm iron push bar, reversible for left- or right-hand doors, on our own tooling.
  • 311 panic exit device900mm bar, lock body with 72mm and 92mm center distances, brass cylinder and stainless handle in the set.
  • 305 panic exit device for fire doors1040mm single-point push bar with outside lever trim.
  • LC14 security door lock case85mm center distance, 50mm backset, euro profile cylinder, four round bolts.
  • 564 night latch and rim lockZinc alloy, 60mm backset, for 35–55mm doors, reversible left or right hand.
  • →Master key and construction key cylinder systemsPlanned from your door schedule.

Who we are

Canton Hyland has made door hardware in Xiaolan, Zhongshan, since 1998, with a quality system certified to ISO 9001 since 2002. Much of what we make leaves the factory under our customers' brands: bring a drawing or a sample and we tool it. Most models start between 300 and 5,000 pieces, and a sample is charged and credited against the first production order.

Not going to Munich?

Ask for samples or a quotation as usual. Everything we bring to the stand is on this site with its specifications.
