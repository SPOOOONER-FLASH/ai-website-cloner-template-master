Ordering and samples

Can I order through Alibaba instead of by email?
Yes. Our verified storefront carries the same catalogue and is often the faster route if you already buy through Alibaba — trade assurance, messaging and payment are handled there. For project schedules, custom finishes or master key systems, email is usually better because those need a technical conversation first.

Products and specification

Do you provide dimensional drawings?
Dimensional drawings are available for most products on request. Send the model number and we will reply with what we hold for it. Where a drawing is not yet published on this site, it is because we have not verified it against the current production tooling — not because it does not exist.
Can you supply master key and construction key systems?
Yes. Master key and construction key systems are a long-standing specialism. These are planned per project rather than ordered from a catalogue, so start with the door schedule and we will work back from it.
Which finishes are available?
Available finishes are listed on each product page under Configuration. Where a product page shows no finishes, that information has not yet been verified for that model and we would rather leave it blank than guess.

Standards and certification

Is Canton Hyland ISO 9001 certified?
Yes. The company has held ISO 9001 certification since 2002.
Are your panic exit devices EN 1125 certified?
Test reports exist for specific models rather than for the range as a whole. We publish each report with the exact model it names, and we do not extend a certificate from one model to a sibling product. Ask about the model you intend to specify and we will send the report that covers it, or tell you plainly if there is not one.
Can you supply test reports and certificates for a tender?
Yes. Tell us the models on the schedule and we will send the reports that name them, with their issue dates and scope.

The company

Where are your products manufactured?
In Guangdong, China. Canton Hyland Hardware (Group) Co., Ltd. has manufactured commercial and residential door hardware since 1998.
