Column · Fire doors

Why a double fire door needs a coordinator

Two closers and no coordinator means the leaves close in whichever order the springs decide. If the active leaf lands first the door never latches — and an unlatched fire door is not a fire door.

Coordinated range 55–85kg per leaf
