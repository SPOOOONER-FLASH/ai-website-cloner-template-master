34:["$","p","16",{"children":"Once the listing has settled the fire question, the choice between knob and lever is about the people who use the door. The usual mounting range for door hardware under those rules is 34 to 48 inches (864 to 1219 mm) above the finished floor. German accessibility guidance, DIN 18040, likewise advises against knobs.
